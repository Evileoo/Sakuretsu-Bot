---
title: ⚖️ Warns and bans
tags: Kage & BroKage, Villages
---
The bot remembers the warns and bans of your village with the **in-game ID** of the player, so it works even if he is not on the Discord server.

- {{cmd:village warn}} : in-game ID, in-game name and reason of the warn
- {{cmd:village ban}} : same, and removes the player from the village if he is in it. A Kage or a BroKage can't be banned, and a player can only be banned once
- {{cmd:village unban}} : pick the player from the list
- {{cmd:village sanctions}} : list of the warns and bans of the village, with pages. Filter it by player or by type

A banned player can't apply to your village.
Warns and bans are written in **flow** or in **moderation**, depending on the flow panel (see {{post:village-management}}).
