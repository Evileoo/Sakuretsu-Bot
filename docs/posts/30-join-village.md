---
title: 🏘️ Join or leave a village
tags: Members, Villages
---
## Ask to join a village — {{cmd:my village apply}}
Pick the village, and add a message for its Kage if you want. The Kage and the BroKages of the village receive your application and answer it. You get their answer by private message.
- One application at a time: cancel it with {{cmd:my village cancel}} to apply somewhere else
- You can't apply to a village that banned you

## Accept an invitation
A Kage can also invite you: the invitation appears in {{channel:villages}}. Click **Accept** or **Deny**, only you can click it.

## In the village
You get the role of the village, access to its channels, and its tag in your nickname.

## Leave your village — {{cmd:my village leave}}
Confirm with the button. You get the {{role:lone}} role back.
If you are the Kage, the BroKage who has been on the server for the longest time becomes the new Kage.
