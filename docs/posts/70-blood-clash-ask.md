## Ask for help
Write a message like this one in {{channel:bloodClash}}:
`help bc floor 756`
with your starting floor. The bot creates your request and adds it to the list of the helpers.
- One request for the whole Blood Clash: only write again if your floor changes, it updates your request
- Give your name, your in-game ID and your schedule ({{post:profile}}, {{post:schedule}}): helpers pick these requests first
- Don't ping anyone in this channel

## Join an open lobby
When a helper opens his lobby, a message with a **Join lobby** button appears in {{channel:bloodClash}}. Click it if the lobby can take you higher than your floor.

## Rules
- Requests are only allowed in {{channel:bloodClash}}. Anywhere else they are deleted
- Repeated requests elsewhere lead to timeouts (10 minutes, then 1 hour), then to the {{role:restriction}} role
- Every request and lobby is reset when a Blood Clash starts
