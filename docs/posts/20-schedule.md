Your schedule is the time of the day when you are usually available to play. It gives the ✅ / ❌ at the end of your nickname, and helps your Kage and the Blood Clash helpers to know when they can find you.

**By default you are never available**: only give the times when you are.

## 1. Give your timezone
{{cmd:my timezone}}, see {{post:profile}}. Your schedule doesn't work without it.

## 2. Give your availabilities — {{cmd:schedule edit}}
- `day` : one day, or *Weekdays*, *Week-end*, *Every day*
- `hours` : `HH:MM HH:MM`, the start and the end, in **your** time

Several periods in the same day are separated by a comma:
`07:00 11:00, 13:00 18:00` → available from 7 to 11 and from 13 to 18
A period can go past midnight: `22:00 02:00`

Editing a day again replaces its periods.

## 3. Check it — {{cmd:my schedule}}
It shows your week. Times are converted to the timezone of the person who reads it.
