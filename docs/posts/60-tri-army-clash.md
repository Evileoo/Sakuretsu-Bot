tags: Kage & BroKage, Villages
---
A channel shared by the villages of the same Tri-Army Clash, to get organized together.

## Create it — {{cmd:village tac create}}
*Kage only.* Pick up to 5 other villages, and a name if you want one. All the members of these villages can see and write in the channel.

## Delete it — {{cmd:village tac delete}}
The Kage of the village that created it, or a moderator, deletes it when the Tri-Army Clash is over.
