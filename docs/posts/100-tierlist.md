{{channel:tierlist}} shows the ranking of the ninjas, made from the tierlists of everyone.

## Make your tierlist
Click **Make your own tierlist** under the ranking, then **Global**, **PvE** or **PvP**. A box opens with 5 tiers:
- **G** : the best ninja of the game (only one)
- **S** : the best ninjas
- **A** : good ninjas
- **B** : meh ninjas
- **C** : bad ninjas

Write **one ninja per line**, the best first. Use the in-game name, or the short name (**Short Names** button).
Add `T2` or `T5` after a ninja when his awakening changes his place: `Kakashi T2`
Skip the ninjas you don't know.

💡 Fill the **Global** one first: the PvE and PvP ones start from it.
Click the same button again to edit your tierlist.
