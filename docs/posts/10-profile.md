The bot needs a few information about you to manage your nickname, your village and the Blood Clash help.

## Your in-game name — {{cmd:my name}}
Give your in-game name **without your village tag**.
> In game you are `AKAI Evileoo` → type `Evileoo`

## Your timezone — {{cmd:my timezone}}
The sign and the hours of your timezone: `+2`, `-5`, `+0`…
Don't know it ? Search *"your country or city UTC time"* on Google, it shows something like **UTC+2**.

## Your in-game ID — {{cmd:my id}}
It helps the Kages and the Blood Clash helpers to find you in game.

## Your nickname
Once you gave your name, the bot manages your nickname on the server:
`AKAI Evileoo ✅`
- `AKAI` : the tag of your village, if you are in one
- `Evileoo` : your in-game name
- ✅ / ❌ : you are available / not available right now, based on {{post:schedule}}
- `(UTC+2)` : shown instead when you gave your timezone but no schedule

Your nickname is updated every 5 minutes, and right away when you change something.
